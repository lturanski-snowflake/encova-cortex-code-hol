# Module 5 (Bonus) — Build a Claims Analytics Streamlit App

**If time allows** — build a live claims dashboard in Snowflake using Streamlit in Snowflake (SiS).

**Paste into Cortex Code:**

```
Use skill .cortex/skills/encova-context/SKILL.md. Build a Streamlit in Snowflake 
app called CLAIMS_DASHBOARD in my GOLD schema.

The app should be a claims analytics dashboard with these sections:

1. HEADER: "Encova Claims Analytics Dashboard" with a subtitle showing total 
   claims count and total incurred amount as KPI metrics (st.metric)

2. SIDEBAR FILTERS:
   - Multiselect for LINE_OF_BUSINESS
   - Multiselect for CLAIM_STATUS  
   - Selectbox for SEVERITY (All, LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH)

3. TAB 1 — "Claims Overview":
   - Bar chart: claim count by LINE_OF_BUSINESS
   - Bar chart: top 10 states by TOTAL_INCURRED

4. TAB 2 — "AI Insights":
   - Bar chart: distribution of AI_COMPLEXITY_CLASS with avg ESTIMATED_AMOUNT
   - Bar chart: distribution of AI_EXTRACTED_DAMAGE_TYPE (top 10)
   - Bar chart: DESCRIPTION_SENTIMENT distribution

5. TAB 3 — "Risk & Fraud":
   - Bar chart: RISK_SCORE distribution (bucketed: 0-20, 21-40, 41-60, 61-80, 81-100)
   - KPI metrics: fraud count, litigation count, high-risk claims count

Query data from GOLD.CLAIMS_GOLD. Use get_active_session() for the Snowpark 
connection. Use st.tabs() for the tab layout.
```
